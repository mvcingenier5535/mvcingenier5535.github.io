## 🎮 How to use the HUD
The HUD activates automatically when you enter any fighter jet model defined in your game files. You do not need to press manual buttons to turn the display on. The instruments show your heading on the top bar. Your speed displays on the left, while your altitude stays on the right side of the screen. 

The weapon status monitor updates every time you cycle through your payload. If you fly too close to the ground, a visual warning cue flashes on the center of the display. This cue stays active until you reach a safe altitude.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting common issues
If the HUD does not appear, check these common points:
* Ensure the folder name in your "resources" folder matches the name in your "server.cfg" file exactly.
* Verify that you restarted your server after adding the code.
* Check your game console by pressing the F8 key while in the game. Look for errors related to the script name.
* Confirm that you are flying a compatible aircraft. Some custom modded jets might not trigger the HUD script.
* Clear your server cache and restart the client if the HUD looks distorted.
